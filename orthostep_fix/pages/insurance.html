Ask our team
Frequently asked

Common questions.

Do you guarantee insurance coverage?

No. Coverage depends on your specific provider, plan, prescription, and applicable rules.

Can I bring an existing prescription?

Yes. Bring your current documentation and we can help explain what information may be relevant to your footwear selection.

Can I ask about the process before visiting?

Yes. Contact the store and we can guide you through the information to prepare.
